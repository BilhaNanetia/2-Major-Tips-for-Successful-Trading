# 2 Major Tips for Successful Trading — YouTube Video Project

This project showcases a video tutorial I created and edited, where I explain two essential strategies for better trading decisions using the **Deriv** platform.

🎥 **Watch the video here**: [2 Major Tips for Successful Tading](https://youtu.be/JnuJPDSk8Uw)

## 📌 Overview

In this tutorial, I cover:

1. **Top-Down Analysis**  
   A method where you begin analyzing charts from higher timeframes (like the daily chart) and gradually move to lower ones (like the 1-minute chart). This approach helps confirm the dominant trend and avoid false signals.

2. **Stochastic Oscillator Usage**  
   This momentum indicator identifies overbought and oversold conditions, allowing traders to anticipate retracements and make informed entries.

The strategies are illustrated using the **Volatility 10 Index**, and the tutorial includes live chart analysis and examples of candlestick confirmations like pin bars, engulfing patterns, and dojis.
